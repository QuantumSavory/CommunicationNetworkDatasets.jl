# Dataset use terms

The source is provided under the [Vermont Open Geodata
Policy](https://files.vcgi.vermont.gov/other/policies/vermont-open-geodata-policy.html).

The policy permits access, copying, sharing, and value-added reuse. It restricts direct,
non-value-added reproduction with intent to sell. This is a custom restriction and is not an SPDX
license identifier. Attribute the Vermont Center for Geographic Information and the Vermont
Department of Public Service.

This artifact is modified: routes are separated by `BUILT` status, exact geometry topology is
derived, identifiers and schemas are normalized, parallel routes are combined, and projected source
lengths are assigned to normalized graph edges as documented in `README.md`.
